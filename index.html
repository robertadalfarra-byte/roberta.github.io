3Where Komoot Is Losing Ground

Feature-level gap (smaller factor)

AllTrails wins on trust-through-community — fresh photos and condition reports, strongest in the US/AU/NZ where Komoot's coverage thins. Outdooractive wins on technical/safety credibility for alpine, off-path terrain.

Trend

These gaps are real but stable — they haven't shifted sharply in the last year. The acceleration in defections traces to a single event, below.

Bigger Factor · Self-inflicted

The Bending Spoons acquisition (2025)

Komoot was bought by Bending Spoons, a firm known for cutting staff and raising prices post-acquisition (Evernote, WeTransfer, Meetup). Roughly 85% of Komoot's ~150-person team was let go within weeks. Shortly before, a previously-bundled feature — device sync via a one-time map purchase — was moved behind a mandatory Premium subscription for new users.

The result: visible community backlash, "enshittification" accusations, and concern over AI training on user data. For a trust-and-community product, this is a bigger threat than any feature gap with AllTrails or Outdooractive.

4The One Feature to Build

If I were PM at Komoot

A live Trail Conditions layer, fused directly into route planning — "Komoot Pulse"

Surface real-time condition reports — surface state, water crossings, closures, recent photos — at the exact waypoint or segment a user is routing through, built on Komoot's existing Highlights infrastructure and paired with its superior elevation/surface data. Not a bolt-on social feed to browse; trustworthy conditions exactly where you're navigating.

Ship it alongside a public commitment that this community data is never used for AI training and stays free for existing users — turning a defensive trust problem into a product feature with a message: investing in the community, not extracting from it.

Attacks the real gap

Targets the #1 reason users defect to AllTrails — trust and freshness signals — head-on.

Plays to strength

Layers onto Komoot's actual differentiator, routing intelligence, rather than copying AllTrails' UI.

Rebuilds trust

Directly counters the Bending Spoons pricing backlash with a visible pro-community commitment.
